# Venture pipeline

Stephen's goal (2026-10-15): start as many businesses as possible and make as much money as possible.
This file is how the network turns that into a steady flow of small, cheap tests. Most get killed fast; the winners get scaled.

## The loop
1. **Scout (Researcher, every Thursday):** take the top unresearched idea in IDEAS.md, or find a better one. Write a brief to `businesses/_scouting/<date>-<idea>.md` with sources, then score it below.
2. **Pick (Strategist, every Monday):** take the highest-scoring researched idea (score 18+). Write a one-page launch plan in `businesses/_proposals/<idea>.md`: the first product or offer, the channel, the cost, what "working" looks like in 30 days, and Stephen's one-time steps. Add it to APPROVALS.md.
3. **Launch (Builder + Marketer, after Stephen approves):** build the smallest version that can take a payment (the MVP), and list it. The aim is one week from approval to live.
4. **Judge (Analyst + Strategist):** after 30 live days, under 3 sales (or the venture's own target) means pivot or kill. A business with sales gets more products and more channels.

## Limits (so quality and usage hold up)
- **At most 2 ventures in build or launch at once.** Live ones that run themselves don't count. More than 2 means half-built things, and the Pro usage limit runs out.
- **Budget:** each test costs under $50 unless Stephen approves more. Every dollar goes in METRICS.md.
- **Stephen's time:** each venture needs at most 30 minutes of his time to set up (accounts, payouts, tax forms) and near zero after that.
- Stephen approves: spending, publishing, new accounts, anything with credentials or legal or tax weight. Nothing launches without his approval.

## Score (each 1-5; 25 max; launch at 18+)
| Factor | 5 means |
|---|---|
| Proof of paying buyers | Competitors visibly selling (review counts, sales numbers, bestseller ranks) |
| Margin | 80%+ kept per sale (digital) |
| AI can do the work | Claude builds and runs nearly all of it |
| Reuses what we have | The spreadsheet engine, Etsy, Gumroad, Pinterest, the agents, existing customers |
| Fast, cheap test | Live within a week for under $20 |

Auto-kill: needs Stephen's ongoing time; needs inventory or shipping; legal or regulatory risk (gambling tips, medical, financial advice, impersonation); no evidence that anyone pays.
