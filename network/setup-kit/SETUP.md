4. **First listing:** use everything from `etsy-digital/cleaning-business-manager/LISTING.md`:

   - Title, description and the 13 tags: copy them exactly from LISTING.md.
   - Category: Templates (search "bookkeeping templates").
   - About this listing: **I did** · **A finished product** · **2020–2026**.
   - Type: **Digital**. Upload files: `Cleaning-Business-Manager.xlsx` and `Quick-Start-Guide.pdf`.

**Policies** (Settings → Policies; Etsy's form, use these answers):

**FAQ** (Settings → Info & Appearance → FAQs):

- *Do I need Excel?* No. A free Google account works.

- *Is this a subscription?* No. You pay once and keep it.
