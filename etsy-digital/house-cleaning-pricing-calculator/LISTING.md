## Description
Know what to charge for every clean before you give the price. Type in the job, and this spreadsheet shows the price, the hours, your labor cost, your profit and your real hourly rate. Then it turns the quote into a customer-ready estimate. Works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no app or subscription.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: enter square feet, bedrooms, bathrooms, add-ons (inside oven, fridge, interior windows, baseboards, cabinets, laundry) and weekly, every-2-week, every-4-week, monthly or one-time service. You get the price per visit, estimated hours, labor cost, profit, margin and your effective hourly rate.
• Target rate check: set the hourly rate you want to earn and a minimum job charge. Low quotes are flagged, and the calculator shows the price that hits your target. No quote goes below your minimum.
• Printable Estimate: your business details, the customer, each line item (service, add-ons, discount) and the total, with a "valid until" date and a signature line. Print it or save it as a PDF and send it.
• Quote Log: record every quote you send. Mark it Won, Lost or Pending and set a follow-up date. See your win rate, the yearly value of the work you've won, and which quotes to follow up on today.
• Settings: your services, base prices, hours, size and room factors, add-ons, frequency discounts, labor cost, target rate and minimum charge are all editable.
