## Description
Run your lawn care business from one spreadsheet: quotes, recurring clients, jobs, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: enter lawn size, flower beds, trees and obstacles, add-ons (hedge trimming, weed pulling, fertilizer, mulch, gutters and more) and frequency. You get the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
• Client Tracker: weekly, every-2-week, every-4-week, monthly or one-time clients. The next due date fills in automatically, and overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted so no recurring mow slips through.
• Jobs Log: pick a client from the dropdown and their price fills in. Crew labor (hours × your rate), materials and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every single job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
• Invoice Generator: type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
• Expense Tracker: fuel, equipment repairs, fertilizer and seed, mulch, dump fees, insurance and more, with customizable categories.
• Profit Dashboard: revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of jobs and unpaid balance for every month, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
• Settings: your services, prices, hourly labor cost, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.
