## Description
Run your carpet cleaning business from one spreadsheet: quotes by room, repeat clients, every job, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: pick a service (carpet cleaning for 3 rooms, upholstery / sofa, area rug, tile & grout, move-out clean, commercial carpet; rename or reprice any of them), then enter the number of rooms (a hallway counts as one), staircases, soil / stain level (0 light to 3 heavy) and add-ons (pet odor & urine treatment, carpet protector, spot dye / bleach repair, furniture moving, deodorizer, mattress cleaning). Rooms over the 3 in the base price are added automatically, and repeat clients get your frequency discount. You see the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and what you earn per hour before you say yes.
• Client Tracker: annual, every-6-months, quarterly and monthly clients (homeowners, offices, property managers) plus one-time move-outs. Visits, lifetime revenue, last visit and next due date fill in automatically, and overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted, so you know who to call for their next cleaning. A notes column holds rooms, pets, parking and access details.
• Jobs Log: pick the client and their price fills in (type an override for any job). Tech labor (hours × your rate), cleaning solutions and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
• Invoice Generator: type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
• Expense Tracker: cleaning solutions, fuel, equipment repairs, van / truck-mount costs, protector and deodorizer, insurance, advertising and more, with customizable categories.
• Profit Dashboard: revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of jobs and unpaid balance for every month of the year, plus totals and a tax set-aside estimate.
• Settings: your services, prices and hours, per-room / staircase / soil-level pricing, hourly labor cost, mileage rate, add-ons, frequency discounts, techs and payment methods are all editable.
