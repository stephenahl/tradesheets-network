Airbnb Host Business Manager

Quick start guide · Google Sheets & Excel

Open it

Excel: double-click Airbnb-Host-Business-Manager.xlsx.
Google Sheets: go to drive.google.com → New → File upload → choose the file → open it → File › Save as Google Sheets. Work in the Google Sheets copy.

Set up (10 minutes)

  1. Settings: business name, tax set-aside %, weekly and monthly discounts, check-in/out times, and your booking channels with their host fee % (check the fee on your own payouts). Expense categories and cleaner names are editable too.
  2. Properties: one row per listing: weeknight and weekend rate, cleaning fee, what you pay your cleaner per turnover, guests included and extra-guest fee. Set Listed since if the listing started this year, so occupancy is fair.
  3. Delete the rows marked EXAMPLE in Properties, Bookings and Expenses.

Every booking

  • Bookings: enter check-in, check-out, property, guest, channel and number of guests. Rent (weekday/weekend aware, with your stay discount), cleaning fee, extra-guest fees, platform fee, payout and net after cleaning fill in.
  • Got the real payout from your platform? Type it in Actual payout. It replaces the estimate.
  • Use Nightly rate override for a custom price, Other fees / discounts for pet fees or discounts (use a minus for a discount), and Status = Cancelled to take a booking out of the totals.
  • Direct booking not paid yet? Set Paid? = No. It shows on the Dashboard until it's paid.
  • The Check column warns you about double bookings and check-out dates before check-in.

Every week

  • Turnovers: your cleaning schedule. Every upcoming check-out with the next check-in at that property, the next guest count, and alerts for same-day and next-day turnovers. Assign the cleaner and tick Clean done? in Bookings.
  • Supplies: count what's on hand after a turnover. Items at or below the reorder level turn red with the quantity and cost to order.
  • Expenses: log bills and purchases. Pick the property, or leave it blank for costs shared by all properties.

When you need it

  • Pricing Calculator: price a stay before you accept it (net to you and net per night), and find your break-even nightly rate from your monthly costs and expected occupancy.
  • Dashboard: type the year. Payout, profit, occupancy, ADR and RevPAR by month and by property.
  • Monthly Summary: pick one property (or All properties) for its month-by-month numbers, stays, average stay, cleaning fees and platform fees.
Color key: yellow cells with blue text are yours to type in. White cells calculate automatically, so don't type over them. Grey helper columns at the far right of Bookings feed the dashboard; leave them alone.
Tip: after you start, clear a booking row instead of deleting it, so Booking IDs stay the same.
