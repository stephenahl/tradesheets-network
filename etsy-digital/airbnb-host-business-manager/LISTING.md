## Description
Run your short-term rental from one spreadsheet: bookings, payouts, cleaning turnovers, expenses, supplies and profit for every property. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Bookings Log: enter check-in, check-out, property, guest and channel (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, direct). Nights, rent (weeknight and Fri/Sat rates, with weekly and monthly discounts), cleaning fee, extra-guest fees, platform fee, payout and your net after cleaning fill in. Type the real payout from your platform to replace the estimate. Double bookings are flagged.
• Turnover Schedule: every upcoming check-out with the next check-in at that property, the next guest count, the cleaner and a done tick. Same-day and next-day turnovers are highlighted.
• Profit Dashboard: payout, cleaning costs, expenses and profit by month, plus occupancy, ADR (average daily rate) and RevPAR for each property. Stays that cross two months are split by nights, so every month is accurate.
• Monthly Summary: drill into one property: nights, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, stays, average stay, cleaning fees collected and platform fees, month by month.
• Pricing Calculator: price any stay before you accept it and see the net to you and net per night. Find the nightly rate you need to break even, or to hit a profit target, from your monthly costs and expected occupancy.
• Properties: up to 20 listings, each with its own rates, cleaning fee, cleaner pay, guests included and extra-guest fee. Shows whether each one is occupied, vacant or turning over today.
• Expense Tracker: per property or shared, with host categories (mortgage interest or rent, utilities, cleaning, supplies, repairs, furnishings, software and more).
• Supplies Tracker: par levels for toilet paper, coffee, soap and more. Low items turn red with the quantity and cost to reorder.
• Settings: channels and their fee %, discounts, check-in and check-out times, categories and cleaners are all editable.
