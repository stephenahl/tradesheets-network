## Description
Run your pet sitting or dog walking business from one spreadsheet: quotes, regular clients, visits, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: pick a service (30 or 60 minute walks, drop-in visits, overnight stays, puppy visits, pet taxi), then enter travel miles, number of pets, medications or special-care tasks, add-ons (holiday fee, extra 15 minutes, litter box, plants and mail, bath and brush, training) and frequency. You get the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
• Client Tracker: daily walks, weekly, every-2-week, monthly or one-time clients, with a notes column for pets, vet and access details (lockbox code, meds, feeding). The next due date fills in automatically, and overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted so no regular gets missed.
• Visit Log: pick a client from the dropdown and their price fills in. Sitter pay (hours × your rate), supplies and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every visit. Unpaid visits turn red.
• Invoice Generator: type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
• Expense Tracker: treats and supplies, fuel, leashes and gear, insurance and bonding, background checks, booking app fees and more, with customizable categories.
• Profit Dashboard: revenue, visit costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of visits and unpaid balance for every month, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
• Settings: your services, prices, hourly sitter pay, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, sitters and payment methods are all editable.
