## Description
Run your snow removal business from one spreadsheet: quotes, per-push and seasonal clients, every push, invoices, expenses and profit for the whole season. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: pick a service (driveway plow, driveway snow blowing, sidewalk & steps, commercial lot plow, salting / de-icing, roof snow removal), then enter the area to clear, snow depth tier, obstacles (parked cars, gates, tight turns), add-ons (salt / ice melt, sidewalk shoveling, steps & porch, mailbox & hydrant clearing, snow stakes, snow pile haul-away) and contract type. You get a per-push price, a season contract price (per push × your average pushes per season), estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate.
• Client Tracker: per-push (on call), seasonal contract or one-time clients, with a notes column for stakes, parking and access. Pushes, lifetime revenue, last push and unpaid balance fill in automatically, and clients who owe you are highlighted so no push goes unbilled.
• Push Log: after each storm, log one row per push. Pick the client from the dropdown and their price fills in. Crew labor (hours × your rate), salt and materials and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every push. Unpaid pushes turn red.
• Invoice Generator: type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
• Expense Tracker: salt and ice melt, fuel, plow and blade repairs, blowers and shovels, snow stakes, insurance, truck and more, with customizable categories.
• Season Dashboard: runs July to June, so one winter stays together (type 2026 for the 2026-27 season). Revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of pushes and unpaid balance for every month, plus season totals and a tax set-aside estimate.
• Settings: your services, prices, hourly crew pay, mileage rate, average pushes per season, add-ons, contract discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.
