## Description
Run your auto detailing business from one spreadsheet: quotes, recurring clients, jobs, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: enter vehicle size, pet hair or heavy soil, travel distance, add-ons (engine bay, headlight restoration, odor treatment, clay bar and more) and frequency. You get the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
• Client Tracker: weekly, every-2-week, monthly, quarterly or one-time clients. The next due date fills in automatically, and overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted so maintenance customers come back on schedule.
• Jobs Log: pick a client from the dropdown and their price fills in. Labor (hours × your rate), products and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every single job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
• Invoice Generator: type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
• Expense Tracker: chemicals and products, fuel, equipment repairs, water and power, insurance and more, with customizable categories.
• Profit Dashboard: revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of jobs and unpaid balance for every month, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
• Settings: your services, prices, hourly labor cost, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.
