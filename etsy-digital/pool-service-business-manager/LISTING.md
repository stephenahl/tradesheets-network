## Description
Run your pool service business from one spreadsheet: quotes, route clients, visits, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quote Calculator: pick a service (full service visit, chemical-only visit, pool opening, closing / winterizing, green-to-clean recovery, acid wash), then enter pool size in gallons, extra bodies of water (spa or wading pool), debris level, add-ons (tile line scrub, extra chemicals, salt cell cleaning, filter cartridge clean, phosphate treatment, equipment check) and frequency. You get the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
• Client Tracker: twice-a-week, weekly, every-2-week, monthly or one-time pools, with a notes column for gate codes, pool size and equipment. The next due date fills in automatically, and overdue and due-soon pools are highlighted so no route stop gets missed.
• Visit Log: pick a client from the dropdown and their price fills in. Tech labor (hours × your rate), chemicals used and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every visit. Unpaid visits turn red.
• Invoice Generator: type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
• Expense Tracker: chemicals, fuel, equipment and parts, test kits and reagents, insurance, truck, route software and more, with customizable categories.
• Profit Dashboard: revenue, visit costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of visits and unpaid balance for every month, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
• Settings: your services, prices, hourly tech pay, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, techs and payment methods are all editable.
