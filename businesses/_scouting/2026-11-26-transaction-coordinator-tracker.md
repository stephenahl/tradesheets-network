# Scouting: Real estate Transaction Coordinator (TC) tracker spreadsheet

Researcher, 2026-11-26 (Thursday scouting). Idea from IDEAS.md (found 2026-10-29). Spend $0. Etsy pages: 1 search page read, 1 listing page refused (permission not answered in an unattended run).

## What it is
A per-deal contract-to-close tracker for real estate TCs and solo agents: pipeline of active files, contingency and deadline dates calculated from contract date (inspection, appraisal, financing, closing), per-file checklist, client-facing status view, fee/income log per file. Excel + Google Sheets, built on our engine.

## Competition
- Etsy: crowded and ad-driven, but the leaders are **Google Sheets only**; no Excel + Sheets TC tracker seen on page 1. Prices on page 1 for spreadsheet trackers cluster at **$24-$25 (sale)**, far above our trade prices ($6.99-$14.99).
- Software: Paperless Pipeline, ListedKit, Open to Close, Dotloop, SkySlope, Brokermint (subscriptions or brokerage-paid). Paperless Pipeline gives away a free deal-tracker spreadsheet as a lead magnet; Jotform has a free table template. Free options cap the low end, but paid Etsy listings still sell at $25.
