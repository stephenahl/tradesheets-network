# Scouting brief: per-trade bid proposal templates (Word + Google Docs + PDF)
Researcher, 2026-11-19 (Thursday scouting). 4 Etsy pages read one at a time, rest by web search. $0 spent.

## The idea
One docx generator makes a bid/proposal template per trade (lawn and landscape maintenance, pressure washing, window cleaning, etc.): cover, scope of work, schedule, pricing table matching each trade's Quote Calculator, terms, acceptance block. Sold standalone or as bundle files inside the trade managers.
Source of the idea: 2026-11-17 brief (MayberryUnlimited cleaning proposal, 203 reviews, 28.3k shop sales at $2.83-$4.71).

## Recommendation (confidence: medium)
Do not launch as its own venture or as 11 standalone listings. Best use: a bundle file inside each trade manager and in the planned Commercial Cleaning Bid Kit (adds perceived value at no listing cost). Build only after the 11-30 kill-rule check shows listings get traffic.

The bigger finding is the benchmark: two new AI-built trade-template shops (TradeTemplatesCo, Wareloft) show 11 sales each after 3-5 months with no reviews. That is a realistic pace for a new shop; our 0 sales in 5 weeks is not yet far off it, but our views (1/wk) are the gap. Worth weighing in the 11-30 check.
