# Scouting brief: Landlord / Long-Term Rental Manager spreadsheet

Researcher, 2026-10-27 (Tuesday run; no [researcher] task in Ready, so scouting mode per role file). Cost $0. Etsy pages: 4 attempted one at a time (2 loaded, 2 failed with client_error and were not retried).

## The idea
A landlord spreadsheet (Excel + Google Sheets) for 1-20 long-term rental units: Properties/Units, Tenants & Leases (lease end alerts, deposit held), Rent Ledger (due vs paid, late fees, unpaid balance per tenant), Expenses by IRS Schedule E category, Mileage, Repairs/Maintenance log, per-property and portfolio Dashboard, year-end Schedule E summary (totals by line, labelled "for your tax preparer", not tax advice). $14.99, Etsy + Gumroad. Built from the Airbnb Host Business Manager pipeline (properties, multi-property dashboard, expense categories already exist).

## Demand signals (real evidence)
- Etsy search "rental property spreadsheet": **5,000+ results** (market page, 2026-10-27). First 6 unique cards show counts of 12.8k, 11.3k, 3.5k, 3.4k, 462, 30 (unlabelled; likely shop review counts, same caveat as the Airbnb brief). All 6 cards were ads, so sellers pay to compete here. https://www.etsy.com/market/rental_property_spreadsheet
- QuickSheetCo "Landlord Rental Property Spreadsheet Template Google Sheets": $5.25, **26 reviews, 5.0**, shop **7.1k sales**, 328 shop reviews (4.7). Google Sheets only (PDF of links), no Excel. https://www.etsy.com/listing/1294366220/landlord-rental-property-spreadsheet
- Many other landlord listings surfaced in search (Excel trackers, rent ledgers for up to 10 properties, Excel + Google bundles): e.g. https://www.etsy.com/listing/1187886391/rent-payment-tracker-in-excel-rental , https://www.etsy.com/listing/1202311711/ultimate-rental-property-trackers-excel , https://www.etsy.com/listing/1136934400/rental-property-tracker-excel-template
- Market size: ~17.7M individual US landlords (Census Rental Housing Finance Survey, via VerticalRent); ~41% own one property; median Schedule E gross rent ~$18.2k/yr (IRS SOI, via VerticalRent). https://www.verticalrent.com/blog/landlord-statistics-2026
- ~61% of independent landlords use some management software (Buildium 2024, via VerticalRent), so roughly 39% don't: the spreadsheet audience.

## Competition and price
- Etsy prices seen: $3.67-$24.96 (most on sale; list $7-$36). Crowded, heavy discounting, ad-driven page 1.
- Free software is the real competitor: Stessa Essentials $0 (paid $12-35/mo), Baselane Core $0 (paid $20/mo). https://www.landlordstudio.com/blog/baselane-vs-stessa . Spreadsheet buyers are the ones who don't want bank-linking apps or subscriptions; listing copy should say "no subscription, no bank login".

## What buyers complain about
- Not enough data: the 4 reviews shown on the QuickSheetCo listing are all 5-star ("Very easy to use, well set up"). No complaint evidence gathered this run (the 2 other Etsy pages failed). Our gaps vs that listing: it is Google Sheets only and has no tenant/lease or rent-ledger tab (summary, monthly income/expense, mileage, IRS categories only).

## Timing
- Hypothesis, not measured: landlord bookkeeping searches should rise Jan-Apr (Schedule E tax season). Listing by early December would catch it. No trend data pulled.

## Recommendation
Launch candidate, **confidence medium**. Paying buyers clearly exist, but the niche is crowded and ad-driven, and our shop has 0 sales on 8 listings: traffic, not product count, is the current bottleneck. Treat it as a sibling of the Airbnb product (same engine, cross-sell "for hosts and landlords"), not a separate business. Suggested order for Strategist: Etsy Seller first (already proposed), then Landlord in time to list by early December for tax season. If the 2026-11-13 kill review says pivot away from Etsy, drop this too.
