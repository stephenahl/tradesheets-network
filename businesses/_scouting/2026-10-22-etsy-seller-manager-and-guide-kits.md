# Scouting brief: Etsy Seller Business Manager (new, 24/25) and trade guide kits (re-scored 22)
Researcher, 2026-10-22 (Thursday scouting). Web search first; 5 Etsy listing pages read, one at a time. $0 spent.

Board task: scout the next venture (guide kits 22, dashboards 21). I researched guide kits first; the evidence was thin at item level, and a search on small-business spreadsheets turned up a much stronger idea, written up as the main brief.

## 1. Etsy Seller Business Manager (spreadsheet): NEW, score 24/25
**Product:** "Etsy Seller Business Manager": Excel + Google Sheets. Orders (paste-in from Etsy's order export), automatic Etsy fee breakdown per order, product pricing calculator (materials + labour + fees → price for a target profit), materials/inventory cost, expenses, monthly and annual profit dashboard, sales-tax-collected tracker labelled "record-keeping, not tax advice". $14.99, sold in TradeSheetsShop (Etsy) and on Gumroad, built with the existing engine like the Airbnb product.

### Demand signals (real)
- **Etsy listing 1762253018** "Etsy Seller Bookkeeping Spreadsheet" (HeyMorning): **Bestseller badge, 190 item reviews, 5.0**, 569 favorites, "20+ views in the last 24 hours"; shop **63.1k sales** in 2.5 years. $7.99 on sale from $15.98. [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1762253018/etsy-seller-bookkeeping-spreadsheet-etsy)
- Audience: **5.7 million active Etsy sellers** (Q2 2026, Etsy 10-Q as cited by [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/etsy-statistics-sellers-buyers-gms-fees)); every one pays a fee stack that is confusing to track.
- Many competing products (shows buyers keep paying): [Etsy 993076115](https://www.etsy.com/listing/993076115) (Excel fee & profit calculator), [Etsy 1006036645](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1006036645), [MonsterOne](https://monsterone.com/google-sheets/etsy-seller-automatic-bookkeeping-in-excel-and-google-sheets-o548301/), and free lead magnets from SaaS tools ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/etsy-fee-calculator-spreadsheet)).

### Competition and prices
- Crowded. Typical $7-$16 on "sale". Brand-new entrants this month: [Etsy 4597094291](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4597094291) (profit tracker + fee calculator, Excel & Sheets) and [Etsy 4583388910](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4583388910) (pricing calculator). Not opened; listing IDs suggest Oct 2026, same age as ours.
- Gap: the bestseller is **Google Sheets only** ("not compatible with Excel, Numbers"); its own FAQ warns of view-only and mobile-app download problems. We ship Excel + Google Sheets and test both.

### Fee facts the build needs (US, as of June 2026, third-party summary; Builder must re-check on Etsy's fee page)
Listing $0.20 (renews per extra unit sold); transaction 6.5% of item + shipping + gift wrap (not US sales tax); payment processing 3% + $0.25; Offsite Ads 15% (12% at $10k+/yr, cap $100/order); currency conversion 2.5%; Etsy Ads optional per click; Etsy Plus ~$10/mo. [Checkout Page](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees). Make every rate an editable setting (rates change, non-US sellers differ).

### What buyers complain about
- Bestseller's visible reviews are all positive ("so easy to set up with detailed instructions"). Pain points come from the seller FAQ: Google Sheets glitches, view-only copies, can't download via the Etsy app. Low data on complaints: confidence low on pain points.

### Score
| Proof | Margin | AI does it | Reuse | Fast test | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 (bestseller, 190 reviews, 63.1k-sale shop) | 5 (~87%) | 5 (spreadsheet + guide + images, same pipeline) | 4 (engine, Etsy shop, Gumroad; but off-brand for trades and needs a maker audience on Pinterest) | 5 ($0, 1 free listing credit, one Builder run) | **24** |

No auto-kill: no inventory, no owner time; tax tracker must say "not tax advice".

### Recommendation
**Launch candidate for Monday's pick (24/25), confidence medium.** Proven paying demand, and we are Etsy sellers ourselves, so the fee logic is something we already track. Main risk is the same as all our listings: a new 0-review listing in a crowded search. Respect the 2-ventures-in-build limit: Airbnb is built and waiting only on Stephen's publish, so this can start once Airbnb is live. "Working" in 30 days = 3+ sales or 300+ views.

## 2. Trade "start/run a business" guide kits: re-scored 22 (unchanged), add-on not venture
### Evidence
- Etsy keyword "cleaning business": **~9,900 searches/mo** (US 55%), ~16-17k competing listings, median price $9.72, 54% under $10; top listings carry unlabeled counts up to 1,614 (third-party estimate, unverified). [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/cleaning-business)
- The sellers who win are **Canva form/branding bundles**, not guides: [Etsy 1749896733](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1749896733) (forms + flyers + 50 IG posts, $6.29 sale, Canva). BizcraftBoutique 8-form pack: shop **899 sales**, this item 0 reviews, $6.64, Canva. [Etsy 1422923377](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1422923377/house-cleaning-business-forms-bundle-8)
- Guide + forms kit (Innomaid): $6.99, **2 reviews, 2.5 avg**, shop 121 sales in 9 years. 1-star complaint: forms came as links to a paid third-party app, seller refused a Word version. [Etsy 1384845644](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1384845644/cleaning-business-starter-kit-40-page)
- Standalone cleaning quoting tool, $20.84: 0 reviews, shop 0 sales. [Etsy 4519345900](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4519345900/cleaning-business-quoting-tool)
- Free how-to guides are everywhere (Housecall Pro, ZenBusiness, Jobber-type blogs), so paid "how to start" text is hard to sell alone. [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/start-a-pressure-washing-business/), [ZenBusiness](https://www.zenbusiness.com/start-pressure-washing-business/)

### Score
Proof 3 (category demand real, guide-type items weak) · Margin 5 · AI 4 (forms in Word/Google Docs/PDF fine; Canva-style design weaker) · Reuse 5 · Fast 5 = **22**.

### Recommendation
Don't launch as its own venture. Better use: a **"Forms & Pricing Pack" (Word/Google Docs + PDF, no Canva or app needed)** bundled into or sold beside each trade manager: intake form, service agreement (labelled "template, not legal advice"), checklist, price list, plus a 2-page pricing guide that matches the spreadsheet's quote tab. Fits the week-1 signal that pricing pins got the best click rate. Confidence: low-medium. Revisit only if a trade listing sells.
