## The idea
An editable guest welcome book / house manual for short-term rental hosts (Wi-Fi, check-in/out, house rules, appliances, local guide, emergency info), sold on Etsy + Gumroad. We can't build in Canva (needs an account in a browser), so our version would be Word (.docx) + Google Docs + printable PDF, and could be cross-sold with the live Airbnb Host Business Manager.

## Competition and price
- Paid Canva leaders at $6-$35 with thousands of reviews and polished photo-heavy designs; bundles (welcome book + signs + social) at $35-$47.
- **Free Word/Google Docs versions are everywhere**: thegoodocs.com (several Airbnb welcome book designs, free), nowistay.com (free PDF/Word), template.net. A Word/Docs buyer can get one free in one search; a Canva buyer pays for design.
- Digital guidebook apps compete at the top end: Touch Stay ~$99/yr per property, Hostfully ~$89-129/yr (rakidzich.com 2026 comparison, unsourced figures). [article](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/touchstay-vs-hostfully-guidebook-airbnb-2026)

## Recommendation (confidence: medium-low)
Do **not** launch as its own venture now. It's a crowded Canva market where our Canva-free angle competes with free Word/Docs downloads, and the network's bottleneck is traffic, not products (Pressure Washing fell to 2 views/wk). Best use: a **bonus file inside the Airbnb Host Business Manager** ("Guest Welcome Book, Word + Google Docs"), which costs one Builder run and adds a reason to buy that rivals don't bundle. Revisit as a standalone only if Stephen will make a Canva version (Canva is where the paid proof is).
