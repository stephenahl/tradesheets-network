# Launch plan: Airbnb Host Business Manager (spreadsheet)
Strategist, 2026-10-19. Scouting brief: businesses/_scouting/2026-10-15-airbnb-host-manager.md. Score 24/25, confidence medium.

## Offer
One Excel + Google Sheets workbook for short-term rental hosts, built on the existing engine (`etsy-digital/make_trade.py`, new key `airbnb`). $14.99 on Etsy (TradeSheetsShop) and Gumroad, same as the trades.
Tabs: Start Here, Dashboard, Properties, Bookings (nights x rate + cleaning fee - platform fee = payout), Turnovers/Cleaning schedule, Expenses (organisation categories, labeled "not tax advice"), Supplies restock, Monthly summary (occupancy %, ADR, RevPAR per property), Quote/Pricing calculator, Settings.
Gap vs. competitors: most listings are bookkeeping-only ($3.53-$22.96, 5,000+ Etsy results). Ours adds booking log, turnover schedule and a one-page dashboard.

## Channel
Etsy listing (1 free credit; 12 left) + Gumroad product. Pinterest: 5 organic pins (host/Airbnb boards), scheduled with the existing process. No paid ads in the test.

## Cost
$0 (free listing credit, no ads). Counts against nothing in the $50 budget.

## Honest caveat
Evidence of demand is real for the niche, not for our shop: the 5 trade listings have 0 sales and 1 shop visit in the first week, and the public Etsy page shows only 1 of 5 listings active. A new listing with 0 reviews in a crowded search may also get few views. The test is cheap ($0, one Builder run) but traffic, not product, is the open question. Fixing the Etsy active-listings issue should come first.
