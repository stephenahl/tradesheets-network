# Launch plan: Etsy Seller Business Manager (spreadsheet)
Strategist, 2026-10-26. Scouting brief: businesses/_scouting/2026-10-22-etsy-seller-manager-and-guide-kits.md. Score 24/25, confidence medium.

## Offer
One Excel + Google Sheets workbook for Etsy sellers, built on the existing engine (`etsy-digital/make_trade.py`, new key `etsy`). $14.99 on Etsy (TradeSheetsShop) and Gumroad, same as the trades.
Tabs: Start Here, Dashboard (monthly/annual profit), Orders (paste-in from Etsy's order export, automatic fee breakdown per order), Fee Settings (every rate editable: listing $0.20, transaction 6.5%, processing 3% + $0.25, Offsite Ads 15%/12%, currency 2.5%; Builder re-checks Etsy's fee page), Pricing Calculator (materials + labour + fees to price for a target profit), Materials/Inventory cost, Expenses, Sales-tax-collected tracker labelled "record-keeping, not tax advice".
Gap vs. the bestseller (Etsy 1762253018, 190 reviews, 63.1k-sale shop): it is Google Sheets only and its own FAQ warns of view-only and mobile-download problems. Ours ships Excel + Google Sheets.

## Channel
Etsy listing (free listing credit) + Gumroad product. Pinterest: 5 organic pins on a "Etsy seller tools" board, scheduled with the existing process. No paid ads (spending is cut).

## Cost
$0 (free listing credit, no ads). Nothing from the $50 budget.

## Build plan (starts only after Stephen approves)
1. Builder: add `etsy` to make_trade.py, build workbook, guide, 7 images, LISTING.md (1 run). Hand-check one order's fee breakdown and one monthly profit.
2. QA: full buyer test, including a pasted Etsy-style order export, deleting vs clearing example rows (Airbnb lesson), Excel and Google Sheets.
3. Marketer: Gumroad copy/cover/thumbnail, 5 pins, SEO title + 13 tags.
4. Stephen: approve the Etsy + Gumroad publish and pins (separate APPROVALS items after QA passes).
Venture limit: Airbnb is the only venture in launch (built, QA passed, waiting on Stephen's publish), so this is slot 2 of 2. Pool Service and Snow Removal are products on the live line, not new ventures.

## What "working" means (30 live days)
3+ sales or 300+ views on the listing. Under that: kill or leave dormant.

## Stephen's time
About 2 minutes now (say APPROVED) and 2 minutes at publish. No new accounts, no credentials.

## Honest caveat
Demand is proven for the niche, not for our shop: 6 listings, 0 sales, 61 shop visits in the best week. A new 0-review listing in a crowded search (two brand-new competitors this month) may get few views, so traffic is again the open question. Two products are already waiting on Stephen's yes (Airbnb, Pool Service), so unpublished inventory is piling up; publishing those is worth more than building this.
