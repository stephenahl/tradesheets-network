## Description
Run your window cleaning business from one spreadsheet: quotes, recurring clients, jobs, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

**What it does**
- **Quote Calculator:** enter window panes, stories, French windows, add-ons (screens, tracks and sills, skylights, solar panels and more) and frequency. You get the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
- **Client Tracker:** every-2-week storefront routes, monthly, quarterly, every-6-month or one-time clients. The next due date fills in automatically, and overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted so repeat customers come back on schedule.
- **Jobs Log:** pick a client from the dropdown and their price fills in. Labor (hours × your rate), supplies and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every single job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
- **Invoice Generator:** type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the service, add-ons, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
- **Expense Tracker:** supplies and solution, fuel, ladders and equipment, water-fed pole costs, insurance and more, with customizable categories.
- **Profit Dashboard:** revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of jobs and unpaid balance for every month, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Settings:** your services, prices, hourly labor cost, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.
