## Description
Ten complete business spreadsheets, one for each service trade, for less than the price of three. Each one handles quotes, recurring clients, jobs, invoices, expenses and profit, and works in Google Sheets and Excel with no subscription or app.

**Who it's for**
- Owners who offer more than one service (cleaning plus windows, lawn care plus snow removal, pool service plus handyman work, carpet plus upholstery and tile).
- Anyone who wants each service tracked in its own file, with its own prices and quote factors.
- Coaches, bookkeepers and VAs who set up tools for service businesses.

**What's included**
- **Cleaning Business Manager:** quotes by bedrooms, bathrooms and square feet; weekly to one-time clients.
- **Lawn Care Business Manager:** quotes by lawn size, beds, trees and obstacles; weekly mowing routes.
- **Pressure Washing Business Manager:** quotes by house size, stories and extra surfaces; annual and seasonal clients.
- **Auto Detailing Business Manager:** quotes by vehicle size, pet hair and travel; maintenance clients.
- **Window Cleaning Business Manager:** quotes by panes, stories and French windows; storefront routes.
- **Pet Sitting & Dog Walking Business Manager:** quotes by service, number of pets, medications and travel; daily walks to one-time clients.
- **Pool Service Business Manager:** quotes by gallons, spa and debris level; twice-a-week to monthly pool routes with gate-code notes.
- **Snow Removal Business Manager:** per-push and season contract quotes by area, snow depth and obstacles; unpaid balance per client; July-June season dashboard.
- **Handyman Business Manager:** flat-rate job menu, materials markup, minimum job charge; maintenance clients for landlords and property managers.
- **Carpet Cleaning Business Manager:** quotes by rooms, staircases and soil level, plus pet odor, protector and upholstery add-ons; annual to quarterly repeat clients.
- A Quick Start Guide (PDF) for each one.

**Every spreadsheet has**
- **Quote Calculator:** price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
- **Client Tracker:** next due dates (or, for snow, unpaid balances) fill in automatically; clients who need attention are highlighted.
- **Jobs Log:** pick a client and the price fills in; labor, supplies and mileage are subtracted to show profit per job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
- **Invoice Generator:** type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in. Print or save as PDF.
- **Expense Tracker** and **Profit Dashboard:** monthly revenue, costs, profit, margin and unpaid balance, plus a yearly (snow: season) total and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Settings:** your services, prices, labor rate, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.

**How it works**
1. Download the zip after purchase and unzip it.
2. Open any workbook in Excel, or upload it to Google Drive and choose File › Save as Google Sheets.
3. Enter your settings and clients, then delete the example rows.

**Good to know**
- Digital download; nothing is shipped. You need Excel or a free Google account.
- Default prices and rates are examples. Set your own in Settings.
- The tax set-aside is a planning estimate, not tax advice.
- For use in your own business. Please don't resell or share the files.
- Designed by TradeSheets Co. with the help of AI tools. Every workbook uses only functions that Excel and Google Sheets both support, and every formula was checked before release.
