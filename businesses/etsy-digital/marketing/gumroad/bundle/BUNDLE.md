## Description
Five complete business spreadsheets, one for each service trade, for less than the price of three. Each one handles quotes, recurring clients, jobs, invoices, expenses and profit, and works in Google Sheets and Excel with no subscription or app.

**Who it's for**
- Owners who offer more than one service (cleaning plus windows, lawn care plus pressure washing, detailing plus washing).
- Anyone who wants each service tracked in its own file, with its own prices and quote factors.
- Coaches, bookkeepers and VAs who set up tools for service businesses.

**What's included**
- **Cleaning Business Manager:** quotes by bedrooms, bathrooms and square feet; weekly to one-time clients.
- **Lawn Care Business Manager:** quotes by lawn size, beds, trees and obstacles; weekly mowing routes.
- **Pressure Washing Business Manager:** quotes by house size, stories and extra surfaces; annual and seasonal clients.
- **Auto Detailing Business Manager:** quotes by vehicle size, pet hair and travel; maintenance clients.
- **Window Cleaning Business Manager:** quotes by panes, stories and French windows; storefront routes.
- A Quick Start Guide (PDF) for each one.

**Every spreadsheet has**
- **Quote Calculator:** price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
- **Client Tracker:** next due dates fill in automatically; overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted.
- **Jobs Log:** pick a client and the price fills in; labor, materials and mileage are subtracted to show profit per job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
- **Invoice Generator:** type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in. Print or save as PDF.
- **Expense Tracker** and **Profit Dashboard:** monthly revenue, costs, profit, margin and unpaid balance, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Settings:** your services, prices, labor rate, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.

**How it works**
1. Download the zip after purchase and unzip it.
2. Open any workbook in Excel, or upload it to Google Drive and choose File › Save as Google Sheets.
3. Enter your settings and clients, then delete the example rows.

**Good to know**
- Digital download; nothing is shipped. You need Excel or a free Google account.
- Default prices and rates are examples. Set your own in Settings.
- The tax set-aside is a planning estimate, not tax advice.
- For use in your own business. Please don't resell or share the files.
- Designed by TradeSheets Co. with the help of AI tools. Every formula was built and tested in Excel and Google Sheets.
