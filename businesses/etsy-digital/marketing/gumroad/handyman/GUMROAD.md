## Description
Run your handyman business from one spreadsheet: a flat-rate job menu, quotes with materials markup, clients, every job, invoices, expenses and profit. It works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

**What it does**
- **Quote Calculator:** pick a job from your flat-rate menu (maintenance visit, TV wall mount, drywall patch & paint, faucet / fixture swap, ceiling fan / light install, door hang / adjust, furniture assembly, caulk tub / shower; rename or reprice any of them), then enter your materials cost, number of units (2 fans, 3 doors), difficulty (ladder, old house, tight space) and add-ons (paint touch-up, haul away, rush, weekend / after-hours, trip charge, ladder work). Materials get your markup automatically, repeat-plan discounts apply to labor only, and the price never drops below your minimum job charge. You also see estimated hours, labor cost, profit after labor and materials, and what you earn per labor hour.
- **Client Tracker:** one-time customers plus monthly, quarterly and every-6-months maintenance clients (property managers, landlords, regular homeowners). Jobs, lifetime revenue, last job and next due date fill in automatically, and overdue clients are highlighted. A notes column holds access, tenant and job details.
- **Jobs Log:** pick the client and their usual price fills in (type an override for any job). Log materials you bill the client and what they cost you. Labor (hours × your rate), materials and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
- **Invoice Generator:** type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in with your business details, the client, the job, materials & extras, total and due date. Print or save as PDF.
- **Expense Tracker:** hardware and fasteners, tools, fuel, insurance, licenses and permits, truck and more, with customizable categories.
- **Profit Dashboard:** revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, number of jobs and unpaid balance for every month of the year, plus totals and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Settings:** your job menu, prices and hours, hourly labor cost, materials markup, minimum job charge, mileage rate, add-ons, repeat-plan discounts, workers and payment methods are all editable.
