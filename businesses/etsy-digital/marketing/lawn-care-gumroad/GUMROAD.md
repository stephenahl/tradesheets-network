## Description
Run your lawn care business from one spreadsheet: quotes, recurring clients, jobs, invoices, expenses and profit. Works in Google Sheets and Excel, with no subscription or app.

**What it does**
- **Quote Calculator:** enter lawn size, flower beds, trees and obstacles, add-ons (hedge trimming, weed pulling, fertilizer, mulch, gutters and more) and frequency. See the price, estimated hours, labor cost, profit and your effective hourly rate before you say yes.
- **Client Tracker:** weekly, every-2-week, every-4-week, monthly or one-time clients. Next due dates fill in automatically, and overdue and due-soon clients are highlighted so no recurring mow slips through.
- **Jobs Log:** pick a client and their price fills in. Crew labor, materials and mileage are subtracted to show the profit on every job. Unpaid jobs turn red.
- **Invoice Generator:** type a Job ID and the invoice fills itself in. Print or save as PDF.
- **Expense Tracker:** fuel, equipment repairs, fertilizer and seed, mulch, dump fees, insurance and more.
- **Profit Dashboard:** revenue, job costs, expenses, profit, margin, jobs and unpaid balance by month, plus a yearly total and a tax set-aside estimate.
- **Settings:** your services, prices, labor rate, mileage rate, add-ons, discounts, crew and payment methods are all editable.

**What you get**
- Lawn-Care-Business-Manager.xlsx (8 connected tabs)
- Quick Start Guide (PDF)

**How it works**
1. Download the files after purchase.
2. Open in Excel, or upload to Google Drive and choose File › Save as Google Sheets.
3. Enter your settings and clients, then delete the example rows.
