# Next 10 trades for the Business Manager line: demand vs competition

Researcher, 2026-10-15. Task: rank the next 10 trades (board, 2026-10-14).
Already live or drafted: cleaning, lawn care, pressure washing, auto detailing, window cleaning.

## Method and limits
- **Demand proxy:** number of US businesses in the trade (Census 2022 employer + nonemployer counts, or industry sources). These are real counts, but they measure possible buyers, not buyers of templates.
- **Competition:** what web search surfaced for each trade on Etsy, Gumroad and Payhip, plus 5 Etsy listing pages read one at a time (the per-run limit). No Etsy search pages were read: the fetch tool needs pre-approved URLs, and bulk lookups are banned.
- **Fit:** how much of `make_trade.py` (clients, jobs, quote calculator, invoice, expenses, dashboard) carries over unchanged.
- **Weak spot:** I could not get per-trade Etsy review counts for most trades. The only strong evidence of Etsy buyers is still the cleaning niche (86–97 item reviews on page-1 listings, LOG 2026-10-14). The rankings below are best guesses from real counts, not proven sales.

## What buyers complain about
No complaints found: every trade-specific listing read had 0–4 reviews, all 5-star. Nothing to learn from reviews yet. Signal worth noting: competitors charge $12–30, so $14.99 is mid-range.

## Recommendation
1. **Build Pet Sitting & Dog Walking next, then Pool Service.** Large solo-operator markets, recurring work that matches our workbook, and no full spreadsheet manager seen on Etsy. Confidence: medium.
2. **Snow Removal before November** if the Builder has capacity: low evidence, but seasonal timing and the same buyers as Lawn Care.
3. **Handyman** third: big market, one weak competitor, flat-rate jobs fit.
4. **Hold the rest** (electrician, plumbing/HVAC, junk removal) until the first five listings show views or sales. With 0 sales after day 1, more listings of the same type is a bet, not evidence. The Analyst should report Etsy views per listing (Stats) after 2 weeks so we can see which trades get traffic.
