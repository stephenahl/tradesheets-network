# Why do live listings with views get 0 orders? Pressure Washing vs page-1 rivals (Researcher, 2026-11-03)

Board task (Researcher, 2026-10-29): compare our top-viewed listing (Pressure Washing, 18 views/7 days) with 2-3 page-1 rivals on price, thumbnail, Excel vs Sheets, reviews and title; recommend 3 fixes. Tuesday run, current-business mode.

## Bottom line
- **0 orders from 18 views is not yet proof the listing fails to convert.** Benchmarks: digital downloads convert 5-10% of *visits* at established shops, but new shops (<3 months) sit at 0.5-2%, i.e. **50-200 visits per sale** (listingview.io). We have had roughly 18 views a week. Expected orders so far at new-shop rates: about 0.2-0.7. Exposure is still the main bottleneck (agrees with the Strategist's 30-day pre-read).
- **But the listing is also positioned against the market.** On Etsy's "pressure washing profit" market page, the clear winner is a **"Pressure Washing Pricing Calculator - Quote, Cost & Profit Spreadsheet" at $9.98 with 604 reviews**. The rest of page 1 cluster at **$4.99-$9.99**; only listings with some reviews sit above $15. We are **$14.99 with 0 reviews** and lead the title with "Business Spreadsheet Google Sheets Excel". Our listing did not appear in the first 8 cards on either market page checked.
- Recommendation: 3 free fixes below (title leads with "Pricing Calculator", launch sale to ~$9.99, quote-calculator hero image with "Instant Download" badge). Confidence: **medium** that these raise click-through and conversion; **low** that they produce sales on their own while traffic stays ~18 views/week.

## Comparison on the 5 factors
1. **Price.** We are mid-high for a 0-review listing. The 604-review leader and a 19-review full business spreadsheet both sit at ~$9.98. Sale strikethroughs (50% off) are common on page 1. Etsy sales/discounts cost nothing to run.
2. **Thumbnail (img_1_hero, looked at).** Clean, but it shows the *dashboard*, not the quote calculator buyers search for; the subtitle and table are too small to read at thumbnail size; about a third of the frame is empty background; nothing says "Instant Download / digital file"; and the demo chart shows revenue *falling* Jun→Oct (Oct margin 23%), which is a weak first impression. listifyai: the first photo should show the outcome and state it is a digital download.
3. **Excel vs Sheets.** Not the gap: we already name both (only 1 of 7 rivals does). Keep it.
4. **Reviews.** 0 vs 604/19/7. This is the largest gap and can't be bought; it only comes from first sales. A lower launch price is the usual way new shops get the first ones.
5. **Title.** Ours spends its first words on "Business Spreadsheet Google Sheets Excel". The best seller, and 3 of 7 rivals, lead with **Pricing/Quote Calculator**, the job buyers are actually trying to do.

## 3 fixes (all $0; editing a live listing is publishing, so Stephen approves)
1. **Retitle to lead with the pricing job.** Proposed (≤140): `Pressure Washing Pricing Calculator & Business Spreadsheet, Quote, Invoice, Client & Profit Tracker, Excel Google Sheets, Soft Wash Template`. Swap 2 weak tags (`small business tool`, `business template`) for `pricing calculator` and `pressure wash price`.
2. **Price to the market for launch:** run an Etsy sale of ~33% off $14.99 (shows ~$9.99 with a strikethrough) until the first 5 reviews, then end it. Matches the $9.97-$9.98 anchor while keeping list price.
3. **New hero image:** Quote Calculator screen as the main image, big readable headline ("Price Every Job in 60 Seconds"), "Instant Download · Excel & Google Sheets" badge, less empty margin; change demo data so the dashboard chart rises. Builder can make it via build.py/_engine (same template likely fixes all 10 trades).

Apply to Pressure Washing first (it has the views), keep the other listings unchanged for 2 weeks as the control, then compare Etsy Stats views/visits/favorites. Exposure still needs fixing separately (approval backlog, free pins).
